# Character AI at the Chapter 11 Decision

Independent entrepreneurship teaching case based on the supplied October 12–13, 2026 public opening docket. Prepared October 14, 2026. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Harvard Business School.

## Contents

- `character-ai-entrepreneurship-case.md`: complete case in Markdown.
- `character-ai-entrepreneurship-case.pdf`: reading copy.
- `character-ai-entrepreneurship-case.docx`: editable copy.
- `teaching-note.md`: learning objectives, 90-minute discussion plan, analytical guidance, and exercises.
- `evidence.csv`: 74 claim-level evidence entries with source locators, status, and limitations.
- `docket-inventory.csv`: inventory and SHA-256 hashes of all 30 supplied docket transcriptions.
- `arithmetic-checks.json`: selected transparent calculations; these are not forecasts or an audited financial model.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums of delivered files.

The case deliberately stops at the opening docket. Proposed relief, sworn assertions, contractual terms, allegations, calculations, and author interpretation have different evidentiary status. No later auction or court outcome is assumed. Original PDF links are reproduced from supplied metadata; the Markdown conversion removed original page boundaries, so citations use docket numbers and paragraph/contract locators.

Source archive: `chartech-docket-markdown.zip`; SHA-256 `b912632aaaf3d8b94d01c1ce72593f0d04c843f6aef50d0ec5bf6fef296cfb80`. The 30 entries' metadata totals 765 source pages; this is not an independent PDF-page audit. Docket 27 is a sealed-content placeholder. Source filings and individual creditor/service lists are not redistributed in this package.

## Use

Read the case before the teaching note. The note contains interpretive guidance. Use the evidence register and docket inventory to trace claims back to the supplied source record. Financial comparisons require matching periods and definitions; the case explains why the disclosed engagement and expense figures cannot establish per-user margins.
